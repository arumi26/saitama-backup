# Educational Email Benefits Lookup Reference

Quick-reference list of services that offer free/discounted benefits with verified `.edu` or institutional email addresses. Useful for bug bounty hunters needing free tooling, hosting, and development resources.

## Verified Programs (Working as of 2026)

### 1. GitHub Student Developer Pack
**URL:** `https://education.github.com/pack`
- **Benefit:** Free access to ~15+ developer tools including GitLab Premium, Namecheap domains, DigitalOcean credits, Stripe access, AWS Educate integration, VS Code extensions & more
- **Verification:** Requires `.edu` email + student status check via GitHub Education partnership

### 2. Microsoft 365 Education A1
**URL:** `https://www.microsoft.com/education/products/microsoft-365`
- **Benefit:** FREE Office suite + Teams + OneDrive + Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook
- **Verification:** School email address (`@university.edu`, `@school.id`, etc.)

### 3. AWS Educate
**URL:** `https://aws.amazon.com/education/edu-promo/`
- **Benefit:** $100 AWS credit + learning badges + cloud sandbox access
- **Verification:** Student/instructor email + academic institution validation

### 4. Canva Pro for Education
**URL:** `https://www.canva.com/pro/education`
- **Benefit:** FREE Canva Pro (unlimited templates, stock photos, brand kits)
- **Verification:** Teachers/students with institutional email

### 5. Notion for Education
**URL:** `https://www.notion.so/product/student`
- **Benefit:** FREE Notion Plus (unlimited pages, file upload, team features)
- **Verification:** Student enrollment email

### 6. Adobe Creative Cloud (Student Plan)
**URL:** `https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/plans/students.html`
- **Benefit:** 60% OFF all apps (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects)
- **Verification:** Student email through SheerID or institutional validation

### 7. Figma Education
**URL:** `https://www.figma.com/community/education`
- **Benefit:** Professional licenses for classrooms (free for eligible institutions)
- **Verification:** School email address

### 8. Hover Education
**URL:** `https://hover.com/education`
- **Benefit:** Free domain registration + hosting for educational projects
- **Verification:** Educational institution email

### 9. Stripe Students
**URL:** `https://stripe.com/guide/students`
- **Benefit:** Access to Stripe dashboard for student founders/developers
- **Verification:** Student status documentation

## Quick Tip
Many sites accept alias emails with `.edu` domains (e.g., `youname+student@university.edu`). If direct `.edu` verification fails, try:
- Using your school's official email portal to generate a forwarder
- Checking if programs partner with SheerID, UNiDAYS, or Student Beans for automated verification
