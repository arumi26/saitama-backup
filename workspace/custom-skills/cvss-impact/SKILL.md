# CVSS & Impact

## Where it pays
Defensible severity + impact narrative.

## Signals
Confirmed finding needing a score.

## Methodology
CVSS 3.1 vector (AV/AC/PR/UI/S/C/I/A) + band (Crit/High/Med/Low); map to CWE; argue a realistic scenario (who loses what).

## Validation gate
Score + CWE + one-line impact, defensible.

## Impact & chains
sets report severity.
