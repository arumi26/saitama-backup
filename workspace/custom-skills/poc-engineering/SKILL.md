# PoC Engineering

## Where it pays
Minimal, deterministic, SAFE proof for reporting.

## Signals
Any confirmed exploit/finding.

## Methodology
Native: deterministic (leak not brute), alignment, benign payload.
Web/API: minimal requests, impact demonstrated, test accounts, redacted.
The 10-minute rule: triager reproduces from scratch.

## Validation gate
PoC reproducible in ≤10 min; benign; preconditions documented.

## Impact & chains
ready for the report.
