# Writing the Report

## Where it pays
A clear report reproduces first try → faster, higher payouts.

## Signals
Validated finding ready to document.

## Methodology
Sections: Title · Summary · Severity(CVSS/CWE) · Asset · Pre-conditions · Steps · PoC · Impact · Remediation · Refs. One issue per report; lead with impact; literal repro; redacted PoC.

## Validation gate
Factual, minimal, reproducible; redacted.

## Impact & chains
drives payout.
